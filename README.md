Entry for the make.fun competition [Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000).

## The entry

> 4/5. Charming planet: the dominant species invented doors, then spent centuries knocking on them to check if anyone was pretending not to be home.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief (an alien tourist's outside view first, laugh from the alien angle, originality away from traffic/gravity/money/weather, a real review shape with a rating, tight with the punch at the end). This one won: a 4/5 rating, and the observation is about knocking — a custom few entrants would reach — seen from outside. The alternatives (queuing to watch rain, then paying to stand under it at home; a glowing rectangle selling shoes) leaned on weather, an explicitly obvious topic, or a familiar phone-ad joke.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed `LINE` constant to `entry.txt`, validates length/single-line/non-blank before writing, and reads the file back byte for byte. Running it twice produces identical bytes (sha256 cc725a7eaa0354a3260cdf8c8662ae7517a57571e16027503bbc763f26bc7964).
